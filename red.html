Does downscaled GRACE add anything at level 6?

Two published global products claim to resolve water storage finer than GRACE measures it: GRACE-SeDA at 0.5 degrees and Li and Kusche at 0.25 degrees. This page tests both against the coarse JPL mascon solution they are built from, on 16,397 HydroBASINS level 6 basins, using no wells and no other outside data. Red means a basin failed at least one test. No basin turns green. A basin that passes has only survived tests built to catch obvious failure, which is not the same as being right.

What the test does

Each basin gets a monthly series from every product, area weighted through a 0.05 degree raster of the basin outlines. That is a conservative remap: a basin mean is the sum of cell values times the area of each cell inside the basin, divided by the area inside the basin. Nearest neighbour and bilinear do not conserve mass and are not used.

  • Not resolved. The basin holds fewer than two cells of a product, or more than 95 percent of it sits inside a single coarse mascon. Structure drawn inside one footprint did not come from gravimetry.
  • No departure. The variance of the downscaled series minus the coarse series is under 5 percent of the coarse variance. The product is the coarse series on a finer grid.
  • Weather field. The departure regresses on precipitation, soil moisture, and snow with an adjusted R squared above 0.8. Both products are built from GRACE plus model fields, so a high value is partly expected by construction. Read it as a degree, not as a surprise.
  • Products disagree. The two products correlate below 0.5, or their trends differ by more than the size of the trend. Where they disagree, at least one is wrong, and that needs no outside data to establish.
  • Ranking unstable. The basin moves more than 20 percentile points between the two products' depletion rankings.

What the test cannot do

This produces red only. It cannot say whether a downscaled value is correct anywhere, whether fine structure reflects groundwater rather than total storage, or whether apparent skill in a wet region is real rather than a shared response to rainfall. Those need wells, evapotranspiration or InSAR, and an ablation against them. That is a separate piece of work.

Sources

  • GRACE-SeDA v1, Gou and Soja, 10.3929/ethz-b-000648738, 0.5 degrees, 2002-04 to 2022-12, millimeters, baseline 2004 to 2009 removed.
  • Li and Kusche, 10.5281/zenodo.17265162, 0.25 degrees, 2002-04 to 2025-03, stored in centimeters and converted to millimeters here.
  • JPL mascon RL06.3Mv04 CRI, the coarse reference. Gain factors are deliberately not applied: they are a model-derived correction, and applying one would add the model structure the test is looking for.
  • GSFC mascon RL06v2.0, a second coarse solution, used only to measure how far apart two processing centers already are.
  • HydroBASINS v1c level 6, GLDAS 2.1 Noah, CHIRPS v2.0.
