Coverage

The open record is North America, Mexico, France, Germany, Scandinavia, parts of Brazil and New Zealand. North India, the North China Plain, Iran and the Arabian Peninsula are absent, and those are where the downscaling test found the most. This page cannot speak for them.

Tens of thousands of wells sit in a few hundred mascons, and a mascon is one observation, so every test counts mascons. Mexico comes from CONAGUA; the nearest Mexican well to any well in the other source is 668 metres away, checked rather than assumed.

How a well is compared

Annual depth to water is negated so up means more water, then taken as an anomaly against the well's own mean. Each product's storage is read at the cell holding the well and GLDAS soil moisture, snow and canopy are subtracted at that product's cell size.

A level is a head, not a storage, and converting needs a specific yield that is unknown per well. Nothing converts: every score is a correlation or an out-of-sample R² on standardized series, unchanged by any positive scale factor.

A well is a point in one aquifer; a GRACE cell is a column over thousands of km². A poor correlation can mean the product is wrong, the well is unrepresentative, or a confined aquifer is moving pressure rather than storage. Evidence, not adjudication.
