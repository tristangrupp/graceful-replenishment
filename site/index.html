Total water storage minus GLDAS soil moisture, snow and canopy, averaged over Noah, VIC and CLSM. Slope fitted with annual and semi-annual harmonics. Significance uses an effective sample size for month-to-month autocorrelation.

Limits

A mascon covers about 12,400 km² and neighbours share them. Leakage is uncorrected, so magnitudes are lower bounds. Basins over one fifth ice are withheld from the groundwater layer.

      Marks

      • Hatching. p ≥ 0.05. The record cannot tell the slope from flat, which is not the same as no change.
      • Grey. No value in this layer: under half coverage, or over one fifth ice.
      • Dashed line. Fitted rate. Dotted line. Global land mean.
      • Drop unresolved. Hides both, on the map only.

      Steps

      • Read GSFC mascons, OBP-ICE6GD, GIA corrected, cm to mm, ocean dropped. gsfc_grid.py
      • Subtract the GLDAS three-model mean, aggregated up onto the mascons rather than GRACE interpolated down. 01_global_gws.py
      • Fit slope and harmonics; Dawdy and Matalas (1964) for autocorrelation. 01_global_gws.py
      • Basins on a 0.1 degree grid, series first, then fit. Coverage tracked, never zero-filled. 02_basins.py
      • Glacier share from Natural Earth. 08_glacier_fraction.py
      • Cross-check against the regional run. 04_crosscheck.py
      • Export this page. 06_export_viz.py
