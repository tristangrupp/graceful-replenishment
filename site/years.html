Frames

  • Level. Distance from that basin's record mean, not an absolute amount.
  • Change from last year. Difference of two annual means. 2018 is blank.
  • First year to last. 2026 minus 2018. It uses 8 of the 92 solutions; the fitted rate on the first tab uses all 92, so the two can disagree.

Deseasonalized

Every January in the record is averaged and that average removed from every January, and so on for each month. 2018 holds June to December and 2026 January to March, both dashed in the year buttons.

Limits

  • An annual mean of 7 to 12 solutions still holds their error, and no significance test runs on a single year.
  • A mascon covers about 12,400 km², so small basins share a signal.
