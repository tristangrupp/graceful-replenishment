What deseasonalized means

For each basin, the code averages every January in the record and removes that average from every January. It repeats this for each month of the year. What remains in a given month is the part its time of year doesn't explain.

This matters for annual figures. 2018 holds only June to December, and 2026 only January to March. Averaging raw months would set one year's summer beside another year's winter. Both partial years have a dashed outline in the year buttons.

The three frames

  • Level. How far a year sits from that basin's own record mean. Zero means the mean, not an absolute amount of water.
  • Change from last year. The difference between two annual means. 2018 has no earlier year, so the map is blank on that frame.
  • First year to last. The 2026 annual mean minus the 2018 one. It holds still while the animation runs.

Read the endpoint difference with care

It uses 8 of the 92 monthly solutions: 5 from 2018 and 3 from 2026. It throws away everything between them. The fitted rate on the other page uses all 92.

The two can disagree, and a wet or dry patch at either end is usually the reason. The tooltip and the table show both numbers together.

Limits

  • An annual mean from 7 to 12 solutions still holds their measurement error.
  • No significance test runs on a single year. Read the frames as a sequence, not as year-by-year verdicts.
  • One mascon covers about 12,400 km², so small basins share a signal with their neighbors.
